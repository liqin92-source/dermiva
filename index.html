Before you cleanse.

Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser is designed for oily, combination, sensitive, acne-prone and normal skin. If your skin is highly reactive or you have a diagnosed skin condition, patch testing and advice from a qualified healthcare professional are sensible.
It is designed to remove everyday dirt, excess oil and sunscreen residue. For very water-resistant sunscreen or makeup, you may prefer to use it as the second step of a double cleanse.
The formula is designed for gentle, non-stripping cleansing and to leave skin feeling soft and comfortable rather than tight or squeaky-clean.
Skin is naturally mildly acidic. A pH 5.5 cleanser is formulated to cleanse while respecting that environment instead of relying on a strongly alkaline wash.
Apply a small amount to damp skin, gently massage over the face, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Use morning and night as needed.
