FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser. Cleanse gently. Care deeply.

What skin types is Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser suitable for?

Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser is suitable for oily, combination, sensitive, acne-prone, and normal skin. It is especially formulated for oily and sensitive skin, making it ideal for daily facial cleansing in Malaysia's hot and humid climate.

Will this cleanser leave my skin feeling tight or dry?

No. Unlike many cleansers that leave the skin feeling tight or dry, Dermiva is designed for gentle, non-stripping cleansing. It effectively removes dirt and excess oil while helping maintain skin hydration and supporting a healthy skin barrier — leaving skin soft, clean, and comfortable after every wash.

Is Dermiva fragrance-free?

Yes. Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser is completely fragrance-free, making it a suitable choice for sensitive skin and those who prefer skincare without added fragrance.

What is the pH level of the cleanser?

Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser features a pH 5.5 skin-friendly formula, designed to cleanse effectively while respecting the skin's natural balance and supporting barrier health.

How should I use Dermiva Gentle Gel Cleanser?

Apply a small amount to damp skin, gently massage in circular motions, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Use morning and night as part of your daily skincare routine. Suitable for everyday use.
